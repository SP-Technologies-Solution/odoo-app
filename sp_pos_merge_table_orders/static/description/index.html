Why You'll Love It

When guests move tables, your orders follow.

Groups combine, friends join a table, or a waiter opens a second ticket by mistake — merge them into one order in seconds, straight from the register. Safe, scoped to the same shop and floor, and fully logged.

🔀

Merge From the POS

A "Merge Orders" button appears in the control panel while a table order is open and other mergeable orders exist.

🚩

Merge Badges on the Floor

Tables holding another open, unpaid order on the current floor are flagged on the floor plan, so you spot them instantly.

➕

Quantities Summed

Identical lines have their quantities added together; everything else is appended — no line is ever lost.

🪑

Source Tables Freed

Once merged, the source tables are released automatically — ready for the next guests.

🛡️

Safe & Scoped

How It Works
1
Turn on Merge Table Orders in Point of Sale > Settings for your shop.
2
Open a table order and tap Merge Orders in the Actions panel.
3
Select the open orders to fold in and confirm.
4
Lines are combined into the current order and the tables freed. Done!

A Guided Tour

From two open tables to one clean order.

1

Turn It On per Shop

Enable Merge Table Orders in Point of Sale > Settings — one checkbox, per point of sale.

2

Open a Table Order

Work a table as usual in the register — while other open orders exist on the same floor, merging becomes available.

3

Tap "Merge Orders"

Open the Actions panel and hit Merge Orders — it sits right alongside Split and Transfer.

4

Pick the Orders to Merge

A dialog lists the open orders on the floor — select one or more and confirm with Merge.

5

Quantities Combine Automatically

Identical lines are summed as they fold in — here the Cheese Burger quantity grows as orders merge.

6

One Order, Confirmed

A confirmation shows the orders were merged into this one, with the final combined quantities and total.
