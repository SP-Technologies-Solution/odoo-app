The Workflow
1
Set your Invoice Approvers in Invoicing settings (per company).
2
On a draft invoice, click Send for Approval → status becomes Waiting.
3
An approver hits Approve, or Reject with a reason (back to Draft).
4
Once Approved, the Confirm button unlocks and the invoice can be posted.
Draft → Waiting → Approved
A clear status on every invoice, visible to accountants and approvers alike.
A Guided Tour

From draft to a posted, approved invoice.

1

Configure Your Approvers

In Invoicing > Settings, add the users who may approve customer invoices — per company. Optionally enable Notify Customer to e-mail them once the invoice is posted.

2

Send a Draft for Approval

On a draft invoice, the accountant clicks Send for Approval. A clear To Submit → Waiting for Approval → Approved status bar tracks the stage.

3

Approvers Get an Activity

Every approver receives an "Approve or reject this invoice" scheduled activity, so it never sits unnoticed in an inbox.

4

Approve or Reject

An approver reviews the invoice and clicks Approve, or Reject with a reason — right from the invoice. Only approvers see these buttons.

5

Approved & Unlocked

Once approved, the Confirm button unlocks and the activities close. The chatter logs who approved it and when — a clean audit trail.

6

Posted & Customer Notified

The approved invoice is posted and numbered, and — if enabled — the customer is automatically e-mailed their invoice.
