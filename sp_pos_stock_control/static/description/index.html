A Guided Tour

Configure once, see stock everywhere.

1

Turn It On in Settings

In Point of Sale > Settings, enable Show stock on products, pick the Quantity Shown (On Hand, Forecasted, or both) and the Out-of-stock behaviour — all per shop.

2

Stock Right on the Product Grid

Every product card shows its live quantity. In On Hand / Forecasted mode you see both figures, and out-of-stock items are flagged in red.

3

Pick the View That Suits You

Prefer a single figure? Show just On Hand (or Forecasted), and set out-of-stock to No Restriction, Warn & Confirm, or Block Sale.

4

At-a-Glance Availability

In On Hand mode each card shows one clear number, with out-of-stock products highlighted in red so cashiers can't miss them.
