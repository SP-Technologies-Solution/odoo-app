Set Up in 5 Steps
1
Create a Meta app with the WhatsApp product and a permanent System User token.
2
In Odoo, open WhatsApp > Configuration > Accounts and enter your IDs and token.
3
Paste the Webhook URL & Verify Token into Meta, subscribe to messages.
4
Click Sync Templates to pull your approved templates from Meta.
5
Open the POS and hit Send on WhatsApp. The receipt is on its way. Done!
A Guided Tour

From setup to a receipt on the customer's phone.

1

Assign WhatsApp Access

Grant users a WhatsApp role — User to send receipts, Administrator to manage accounts and templates.

2

Open the WhatsApp App

A dedicated WhatsApp app appears in the menu, guiding you to connect your first Cloud API number.

3

Connect Your Account

Paste your Meta credentials, hit Test Connection, and copy the ready-made Webhook and Meta App-Review URLs.

4

Build a Document Template

A guided wizard creates a document-header template and submits it to Meta — usually approved within the hour.

5

Sync Approved Templates

Pull your templates back from Meta with live approval status — Approved or In Review — right inside Odoo.

6

Track Every Message

The message log lists every receipt sent, with recipient, template and live Sent / Delivered / Read state.

7

Configure Your POS

Turn on Send receipts on WhatsApp per shop and pick which account and receipt template to use.

8

Send at the Counter

On the payment screen, one tap of Send on WhatsApp delivers the receipt straight to the customer's phone.
