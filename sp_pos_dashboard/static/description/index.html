Overview

POS Dashboard provides a comprehensive analytics dashboard for your Point of Sale operations. Track sales performance, monitor top products and customers, analyze payment methods, and evaluate staff performance - all from a single, beautifully designed dashboard. With interactive Chart.js charts, real-time KPI cards, flexible date filters, and one-click Excel export, you get complete visibility into your POS business at a glance.

Key Features

✓ KPI Cards - At-a-glance metrics for Total Sales, Total Orders, Average Order Value, and Items Sold with period comparison.
✓ Sales Trend Chart - Visualize sales over time with daily, weekly, or monthly grouping.
✓ Top 10 Products & Customers - Identify best-selling products and highest-value customers with drill-down support.
✓ Category Distribution - Donut chart showing revenue breakdown by product category.
✓ Payment Methods & Staff Performance - Analyze payment splits and track cashier productivity.
✓ Advanced Filters - Filter by date range, period, company, POS shop, and session.
✓ Multiple Chart Types - Switch between bar, line, pie, doughnut, and table views for each widget.
✓ Excel Export - Export complete dashboard data to Excel with a single click.

Use Cases

→ Store managers can monitor daily sales performance and identify peak hours.
→ Business owners can compare sales across multiple POS shops and sessions.
→ Inventory teams can track top-selling products and plan restocking based on category trends.
→ HR and operations can evaluate staff performance and optimize shift scheduling.

Configuration

1 Install the POS Dashboard module from Apps.
2 Navigate to Point of Sale → POS Dashboard from the main menu.
3 Set the Date From and Date To range, and select the desired Period (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4 Optionally filter by Company, POS Shop, or Session for targeted analysis.
5 Click Apply to refresh the dashboard, or use Export Excel to download the report.
